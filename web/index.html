Bengaluru 3D — Data Sources & Attribution

Built from open map data. This project fuses open geospatial datasets into an interactive 3D WebGL viewer of Bengaluru.

Data Sources & Attribution

  • OpenStreetMap: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Transit routes, track alignment, stations, and street centrelines.
  • Overture Maps: Overture Maps Foundation release 2026-10-21.0 (ODbL / CDLA Permissive 2.0). 3D building polygons and transportation base.

Methodology & Disclaimers

• Solar Position & Atmosphere: Sun elevation, azimuth, colour temperature, sky gradient, and directional shadows are calculated from an analytical NOAA solar model for Bengaluru (lat 12.9716, lon 77.5946, IST UTC+5:30) using the selected date and time. Directional shadows are rendered for chunks within a 2 km quality radius of the camera target.

• Procedural Window Lighting: Window illumination and facade glow at night are procedural shader patterns; they do not represent real-time building occupancy or sensor data.

• Illustrative Road Traffic: Vehicle streams along motorway, trunk, and primary roads are animated procedurally on the GPU as moving light points. India left-hand drive is modelled with white headlights in forward travel and red taillights in reverse travel. Pairs of major road polylines running parallel within 7 m to 40 m (angle < 15°) are detected as divided carriageways and rendered as one-way streams (left carriageway forward with white headlights, right carriageway reverse with red taillights) using a geometric inference, not tagged data. Parallel pairs under 7 m (stacked flyovers or duplicate centrelines) and unpaired segments stay two-way (offset 1.8 m to the left of travel). Polylines crossing chunk boundaries are paired within each 1 km chunk independently. Elevated flyovers are not modelled; vehicle lights follow terrain-draped road geometry. Speeds and densities are illustrative and do not reflect live traffic or sensor telemetry.

• Building Heights & Raster: Heights come from OSM/Overture tags where present, otherwise from a 2018 GHSL raster of about 90 m cells (an average, so tall towers are underestimated). Tags above 165 m (not verified landmarks), or 5+ identical tags of 100 m or more within 300 m, are replaced by the raster value (228 buildings). Tall tags with a low raster value are shown as "OSM tag, unverified" (232 buildings). Buildings with no raster value use an area-based guess (4,994 buildings).

• Namma Metro Network: 102.79 one-way route km operational (Purple 43.48 km, Green 33.86 km, Yellow 18.05 km, Pink 7.40 km); 88 unique physical operational stations in OSM (80 elevated, 7 underground, 1 at grade), 91 when interchange stations are counted once per line. The difference from the public reference of 91 stations is not resolved. Metro trains are illustrative, not live.

• Procedural Hero Landmarks: Prominent landmarks (Vidhana Soudha, UB City complex, and Kempegowda International Airport Terminal 2) feature procedural 3D models built upon real Overture/OSM footprint geometry. Stylised model, not survey geometry. Vidhana Soudha (46 m) and UB City (verified 123 m automatic match to nearest footprint within 60 m; the footprint may be a neighbouring tower of the complex) reflect verified heights. Other towers in the UB City complex use estimated heights (unverified). Kempegowda Terminal 2 height is 12.8 m, matching the data pipeline extrusion derived from 4 tagged floors (4 × 3.2 m).

• Assumptions: Blue, Orange, and Red lines forced to construction; Pink elevated section with start_date 2026-11-14 treated as operational; Pink tunnels treated as construction; viaduct deck height METRO_VIADUCT_M = 8.0 m; underground tunnel depth METRO_TUNNEL_DEPTH_M = -12.0 m (an assumed engineering depth); airport runway width AIRPORT_RUNWAY_WIDTH_M = 45.0 m unless an OSM width tag exists. Two Phase 1 portal ramp ways (Purple Line UG1: 508711042, 607901267) join the elevated viaduct at MG Road / Reach 1 (layer 2) to the underground tunnel at Cubbon Park / UG1 (layer -2); they are schematic transitions (grade ~11.6%, depth is an assumption) classified under portal mode derived from connectivity, rendered as a descending ramp interpolating from viaduct deck height (+8.0 m) down to the assumed tunnel depth (-12.0 m), hidden in default view, with the above-ground portion (+8m to 0m) rendered in default view so the viaduct deck transitions smoothly into terrain. Way 1189861890 is classified via level < 0 tag rule. Green Line portal approach ways 142484399 and 607901295 carry embankment=yes and are retained as surface/at-grade approach tracks.

• Verified Landmarks: Only landmarks with a sourced height are verified: Vidhana Soudha, UB City (UB Tower), and World Trade Center Bangalore.

• Guided Tours: Cinematic camera paths over open city data capped at 75 m/s. Progress is strictly gated on chunk readiness: movement advances only when the terrain and building chunk under the camera and all chunks within 1.2 km along the next 1.0 km of the path are fully loaded; otherwise the tour pauses and displays "Loading...". Station stops display only official name, line, and operational mode (“<name> · Purple Line · elevated/underground/at-grade”). Exiting or completing a tour cleanly restores user environmental and display settings.
