Bengaluru 3D — Data Sources & Attribution

Built from open map data. This project fuses open geospatial datasets into an interactive 3D WebGL viewer of Bengaluru.

Data Sources & Attribution

  • OpenStreetMap: OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Transit routes, track alignment, stations, and street centrelines.
  • Overture Maps: Overture Maps Foundation release 2026-10-21.0 (ODbL / CDLA Permissive 2.0). 3D building polygons and transportation base.
  • GHSL (JRC): European Commission JRC Global Human Settlement Layer GHS-BUILT-H 2018 raster (CC BY 4.0).
  • Copernicus DEM: Copernicus GLO-30 Digital Elevation Model (European Union / ESA). 30 m elevation raster.
  • Rendering: Three.js 3D WebGL library.

Methodology & Disclaimers

• GHSL Epoch (2018) & Corroboration: The GHSL raster reflects the 2018 epoch (~90 m cells). For newer high-rises constructed after 2018, height tags that agree within 20% of their floor-count estimate (floors × 3.2 m, both ≥ 80 m) are preserved and marked as Corroborated by Floors. Tall tags without floor corroboration remain flagged with an Uncertain Height warning badge.

• Namma Metro Network: Viaduct guideways use a standard assumed clearance of 8m (METRO_VIADUCT_M = 8.0) above DEM terrain. The Pink Line elevated section (Kalena Agrahara to Tavarekere, 6 stations, with Jayadeva as a two-level interchange) opened on 13 Nov 2026 and is included as operational. Metro trains are illustrative, not live.

• Verified Landmarks: Only named public landmarks (such as Vidhana Soudha, World Trade Center, Chinnaswamy Stadium) use ground-truth verified heights.
